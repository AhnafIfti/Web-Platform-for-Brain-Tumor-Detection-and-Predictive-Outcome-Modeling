Preprocessing fidelity

The implementation follows notebook cell 16: each slice is normalized as (x − min) / (max − min) when max > 0, cast to float32, stacked in FLAIR/T1ce order, and resized using PyTorch bilinear interpolation with align_corners=False.

One documented repair: positive constant slices become zero to avoid division by zero. No ImageNet normalization, automatic registration, skull stripping, or ground-truth masks are introduced.

The notebook calls this “2.5D,” but the implemented input contains two modalities from one slice, not neighboring slices. The later MONAI 3D experiments are a separate architecture.

Know the limitations

Training and validation selected top-K slices using ground-truth tumor masks. Inference processes all non-empty slices. This changes the evaluation distribution; historical slice Dice does not establish full-volume sensitivity or specificity.

Segmentation probabilities are not calibrated diagnostic confidence. Independent channels may violate expected region nesting; outputs are preserved without undocumented postprocessing.

Inputs must already be co-registered and prepared consistently with the training data. Matching spatial transforms alone cannot confirm that two scans belong to the same subject.

Area statistics are model-grid pixels, not physical tumor burden. BraTS label conventions must be checked for each dataset version, particularly newer releases.
